IMPORTANT

Data & limitations

Local-only prototype: project information is stored in this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data or switching devices may remove or hide it. Export JSON backups regularly.

Client approvals are simulated: the approval preview and approve/decline buttons change only the local demo data. They do not send a link to a client, verify identity, collect a legally verified signature, or create a tamper-proof audit log.

For real client-facing approvals: add a backend/database, secure random approval tokens, server-side timestamps, access controls, audit logging, and a published privacy policy. Never store confidential client material in this public prototype.

Not legal advice: consult a qualified professional for contract wording and electronic-signature requirements in your jurisdiction.
